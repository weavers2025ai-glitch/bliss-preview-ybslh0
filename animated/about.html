Who We Are

One of the leading outdoor furniture brands in the UAE

Bliss Furnishing is one of the leading outdoor furniture brands in the UAE, a proud legacy of Weavers Creation, a trusted name in the industry for over 15 years. Renowned for crafting premium-quality outdoor furniture, we blend superior materials, innovative design, and timeless elegance to create spaces that inspire comfort and style.

Our journey has grown through our own showroom in Indonesia, alongside a strong presence across the UAE and international markets, where we continue to deliver exceptional products and experiences for homes, resorts, and commercial spaces.

Manufacturing Units

Crafted in Indonesia, finished in the UAE

Indonesia — Jepara

Our own showroom and manufacturing facility in Jepara, Indonesia — a renowned hub of craftsmanship. A dedicated team of skilled artisans uses locally sourced teak, rattan, and premium materials to create world-class outdoor furniture, combining traditional techniques with modern designs.

United Arab Emirates — Dubai

Our UAE facility in Dubai serves as a key hub for design, customization, and distribution across the region. With a focus on quality, precision, and customer satisfaction, we deliver premium outdoor furniture for residential, commercial, and hospitality projects.

Market Analysis

A market made for outdoor living

The UAE outdoor furniture market is experiencing strong growth, driven by rising demand for luxury living, premium hospitality projects, and the rapid expansion of villa and resort developments. With a warm climate that encourages year-round outdoor living, consumers are increasingly investing in high-quality, weather-resistant furniture. This creates a growing opportunity for brands like Bliss, known for their premium designs, durability, and craftsmanship, to lead in this expanding market.
